# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Readers of 《资治通鉴》 using an offline-first PWA for sustained classical-history reading: students, enthusiasts, and researchers who value source fidelity but need orientation, place-keeping, and low-friction lookup while reading on desktop and mobile.

## Product Purpose

Provide a serious, source-first digital reader for 《资治通鉴》 with inline 胡三省音注, 卷/year navigation, search, and spoiler-aware lookup. Success means readers can stay immersed in the primary text while getting just enough help to understand where they are, who matters, and why an entry matters.

## Brand Personality

Scholarly, calm, trustworthy. The interface should feel like a well-made annotated edition rather than a study app, chatbot, or flashy learning product.

## Anti-references

Avoid gamified learning UI, AI-chat companion aesthetics, over-decorated cards, dense mobile toolbars that feel like operating a reference console, and any treatment that makes editorial help look like Sima Guang's source text or Hu Sanxing's commentary.

## Design Principles

- Source first: never replace, paraphrase over, or visually compete with the classical text.
- Explain without interrupting: comprehension aids are concise, close to the relevant span, and easy to collapse or disable.
- Label provenance clearly: 原文、胡注、编者导读 must be visually and semantically distinct.
- Preserve reading flow: desktop, touch, keyboard, and offline use should all support long-form immersion.
- Reward curiosity: search, identity, and guide affordances should help readers continue reading rather than send them into another research task.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, screen-reader labels that announce editorial aids as non-source material, reduced-motion-safe state changes, and mobile controls that remain reachable without crowding the reader header.
